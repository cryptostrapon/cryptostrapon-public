# Red-Flag Pattern Categories

High-level categories of scam patterns the detector and investigations look
for. These are descriptive categories for public reference, not the
detector's internal rule set.

## Impersonation
Fake "support" accounts, fake verified badges, or messages pretending to be
from an exchange, wallet provider, or well-known figure.

## Urgency / fear
"Your wallet has been compromised," "verify now or lose access" — pressure
designed to short-circuit careful checking.

## Fake airdrops / giveaways
Promises of free tokens in exchange for connecting a wallet, signing a
transaction, or sending a small amount first.

## Phishing links
Typosquatted domains (e.g. a misspelled exchange name), fake "verify your
wallet" pages, and links that lead to a wallet-drain contract approval.

## Fake hardware / software update prompts
Messages claiming a hardware wallet (e.g. Ledger) needs a firmware or app
"update" via a link, used to harvest seed phrases.

## Wallet-drain approvals
Requests to "sign" or "approve" a transaction that, if signed, grants a
scam contract ongoing access to a victim's tokens.

## Fake support / recovery services
Accounts or sites offering to "recover" lost funds for an upfront fee.

## Mixer / obfuscation flags
Wallet activity routed through mixers or rapid hop chains, used as one
signal among several — not a verdict on its own.
